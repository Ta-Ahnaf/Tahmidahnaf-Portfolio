MY STORY / WHERE IT STARTED

Born in 2003

QUESTIONS
FIRST.
ALWAYS.

Born in 2003, I grew up asking questions faster than anyone around me could answer them. These days Artificial Intelligence keeps that curiosity fed.

After my SSC I completed a Diploma in Computer Engineering, while spending just as much energy on debating, acting, poetry and stage performances. Somewhere between editing silly videos for friends and losing hours to story-driven games, coding stopped being a subject and became the thing I actually wanted to do.

I paused my academic path to explore new experiences, pick up fresh skills, and get ready for the next chapter of my education abroad. My approach is simple: when I love something, I don’t have to force it—I just do it, and I learn fast because of it. When the interest isn’t there, I’m honest about that too.
